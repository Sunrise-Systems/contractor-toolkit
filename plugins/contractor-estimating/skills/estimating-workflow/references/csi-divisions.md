# CSI MasterFormat Division Reference

Construction Specifications Institute (CSI) MasterFormat 2018 division reference. Universal across U.S. commercial construction. Includes division descriptions, typical scope items, and applicability by project type.

The unit cost ranges in this document are **national averages** — multiply by your local market multiplier (see `cost-reference.md`). The relative cost relationships between line items hold across markets even when absolute numbers shift.

## Detailed Division Descriptions

### Division 01 — General Requirements

General conditions and project-wide costs. Not a trade division — these are indirect costs.

| Item | Description | Typical Cost Driver |
|------|-------------|---------------------|
| 01 10 00 | Summary — project description, scope | — |
| 01 20 00 | Price and Payment Procedures | — |
| 01 30 00 | Administrative Requirements | Submittals, meetings, schedule |
| 01 40 00 | Quality Requirements | Testing, inspections, mock-ups |
| 01 50 00 | Temporary Facilities and Controls | Site office, fencing, temp utilities |
| 01 60 00 | Product Requirements | Substitutions, product data |
| 01 70 00 | Execution and Closeout | Punch list, O&M manuals, warranties |
| 01 80 00 | Performance Requirements | Commissioning coordination |

**Typical Cost:** 8-15% of direct construction cost.

## Applicability by Project Type

### Commercial TI (Typical Divisions)

| Division | Likely Included | Notes |
|----------|----------------|-------|
| 01 - General Requirements | Yes | Always |
| 02 - Existing Conditions | Yes | Demo of prior tenant |
| 06 - Wood/Plastics | Yes | Framing, blocking, millwork |
| 07 - Thermal/Moisture | Sometimes | Insulation, firestopping |
| 08 - Openings | Yes | Doors, hardware, glass |
| 09 - Finishes | Yes | Largest cost driver in TI |
| 10 - Specialties | Yes | Restroom accessories, signage |
| 21 - Fire Suppression | Yes | Sprinkler modification |
| 22 - Plumbing | Yes | Fixtures, connections |
| 23 - HVAC | Yes | Ductwork, controls |
| 26 - Electrical | Yes | Power, lighting |
| 27 - Communications | Sometimes | Data cabling |
| 28 - Safety/Security | Yes | Fire alarm modification |

### New Construction (All Divisions Potentially Apply)

All divisions 01-33 may apply. Focus estimating effort on divisions representing the largest cost: 03 (Concrete), 05 (Metals), 07 (Roofing), 09 (Finishes), 23 (HVAC), 26 (Electrical).

### Restaurant / Food Service

All TI divisions plus: heavy 21/22/23 (kitchen hood, grease, exhaust). Division 09 finishes drive cost in dining areas.

### Healthcare

All TI divisions plus state hospital-authority compliance package (e.g., HCAI in California, equivalent agencies in other states). Adds 20-40% to cost and 6-18 months to timelines.

### Industrial / Warehouse

Often simpler: 03 (slab), 05 (steel/PEMB), 07 (roof), 22/23/26 sized for use. Less finish work in production areas.
