# Master Exclusions Library

Standard exclusions organized by service line and CSI division. **Customize this aggressively** — your past proposals are the best source. Every shop has its own boundary language, and the items below cover the most common 60% of what GCs and A&E firms typically exclude.

When generating a proposal, filter this library to:
1. The applicable service line (A&E vs. GC vs. Electrical vs. Mechanical vs. Plumbing vs. Civil)
2. The applicable CSI divisions for the project (from Phase 1)

Never dump the entire library into a proposal. Filtered exclusions read as careful scope-setting; raw master-list dumps read as defensive overreach.

## Standard Qualifications (Always Include)

Append these to every estimate unless specifically removed:

1. Estimate valid for 30 days from date of issue
2. Based on [design phase] level documents; design changes may affect costs
3. Excludes costs for unforeseen conditions
4. Based on current building codes; future changes may affect costs
5. Pricing reflects current subcontractor market; actual bids may vary
6. Permit timelines based on current AHJ processing times
7. Hazmat abatement excluded unless specifically included
8. Based on standard working hours (Mon-Fri, 7am-3:30pm local time)
9. Assumes reasonable site access for construction activities
10. Assumes existing utilities are adequate; temporary services as noted

## Usage Notes

- **Filter, don't dump.** Pull only items applicable to the project's scope and divisions.
- **Customize per project.** Add project-specific exclusions in Phase 6.
- **Harvest from past proposals.** Every time you write a new exclusion specific to a project, evaluate adding it to this master file.
- **Coordinate with sub bids.** Sub proposals bring their own exclusions — flow them through to the owner.
- **Plain English helps.** When the proposal goes to a client (not a sub), translate technical exclusion language to plain English. Disputes start when the client doesn't understand what was excluded.
